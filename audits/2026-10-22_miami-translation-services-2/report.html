Comparison vs. HOME template

Matches HOME: - Same global header, top bar (phone (800) 460-1536, Open 24/7), footer, and certification badges. - Same Lato body / Roboto headings declaration. - Same teal #47ACAD / navy #1F3D5C–#2B4763 palette for brand elements. - CTA labels and destinations follow the style guide: Order Translation → /billing, Get Free Quote / Get Instant Quote → /translations/quotes/. - Brand terms (USCIS, ATA, ISO 9001:2015 / 17100:2015, certified translation) are used correctly.

Deviates by design: this page uses the location-landing template, not the HOME layout. Its section order (hero, price ticker, trust strip, services cards, why it matters, how it works, why choose, access anywhere, testimonials, FAQ) matches the approved Florida standard, so section structure was judged against that standard. Deviations from the standard are findings 23 and 24, plus: - FAQ questions are worded differently ("What documents…" vs. "What types of documents…"). Accepted. - The services H2 says "in Miami" instead of "Throughout {Location}", which suits a city page. - The services subtitle is "Supporting Miami's International Community" instead of "Accurate Translations for Individuals, Businesses, and Organizations". Accepted as an equally specific angle.

Colors outside the HOME palette: the landing template brings in Tailwind-style colors: - peach icon box #FDEEE9 and decorative border #EBD2C5 - orange avatar #C2531A - teal #0D9488 - slate text #94A3B8 and #334155 - classes named terracotta that actually render brand teal

The off-palette hover was fixed (D1), and the two contrast failures were fixed (A3–A5). The other accent colors are kept because they're part of the approved Florida template.

Same defects as HOME: ISO 17100 alt text (fixed here); Spanish avatar alt text (fixed here); brand-teal contrast; Roboto declared but never loaded (headings fall back to Arial on Windows and Helvetica on Mac).

Better than HOME: - The visible copy says 120+ languages throughout, with no "90" contradictions. - The viewport has no maximum-scale=1.0, so pinch-zoom works. - Every price claim matches HOME (10¢ per word).
